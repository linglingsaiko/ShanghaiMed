## Section 2: Acupuncture Shanghai: Ancient Needles for Modern Relief

### The Science Behind the Needles

Acupuncture involves inserting fine needles into specific points to stimulate the body's self-healing mechanisms. Modern studies suggest it may help with **chronic pain, migraines, anxiety, fertility issues, and digestive disorders**.

At Longhua Hospital, practitioners use both classical and evidence-based protocols. Patients often experience reduced inflammation, improved sleep, and a sense of calm after just a few treatments.

### What to Expect

Most treatments at Longhua Hospital follow this process:
1. **Comprehensive consultation** — pulse diagnosis, tongue analysis, medical history
2. **Personalized treatment plan** — typically 6-12 sessions depending on condition
3. **Session format** — 30-45 minutes, combining acupuncture with optional electro-stimulation or moxibustion
4. **Progress monitoring** — practitioner adjusts the plan every 3-5 sessions

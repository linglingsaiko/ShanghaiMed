## Why Choose Shanghai for Your Health Checkup?

Shanghai offers world-class healthcare facilities with international standards, making it an ideal destination for health checkups. The city's leading hospitals provide comprehensive screening packages tailored for international patients.

### Key Advantages
- **Affordable Prices**: Up to 70% savings compared to Western countries
- **English-Speaking Staff**: Dedicated international departments
- **Advanced Technology**: State-of-the-art medical equipment
- **Quick Results**: Fast turnaround time for test results

## What to Expect During Your Checkup

A typical health checkup in Shanghai includes:

1. **Registration**: Quick check-in at the international department
2. **Consultation**: Initial discussion with a healthcare provider
3. **Tests**: Blood work, imaging, and specialized screenings
4. **Lunch**: Healthy meal provided during testing
5. **Results**: Detailed report with personalized recommendations

## Tips for International Patients

- Book your appointment 2-4 weeks in advance
- Bring your passport and insurance information
- Fasting is required for certain tests (usually 8-12 hours)
- Plan for 1-2 days for comprehensive checkups
- Translation services are available at all major hospitals

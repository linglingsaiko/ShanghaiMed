## Step 1: Verify the company is real and registered

Every legitimate business in mainland China is registered with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) and has a **Unified Social Credit Code** (统一社会信用代码) — an 18-digit identifier that is the Chinese equivalent of a business registration number. You can look up any company on the **National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System** (国家企业信用信息公示系统) at [gsxt.gov.cn](https://www.gsxt.gov.cn).

**What to check for any agency:**
- Do they publish their legal company name, not just a brand name?
- Do they provide a registration number that can be looked up independently?
- Is their registered address a real commercial address, not a virtual office mailbox?
- Do they identify a founder or legal representative by real name?

## Step 2: Verify the hospital and the doctor independently

A medical travel agency is only as good as the hospitals and doctors it can connect you to. Any agency can claim partnerships. **You should be able to verify both, independently, before you pay anything.**

### Verify the hospital

All the public hospitals we work with are **Grade 3A (三甲)** — China's highest hospital classification — and every one of them has an official website and an international patient department you can contact directly. You do not need to take our word for it.

Our [Hospital Network page](/treatments) lists each partner hospital. We encourage you to:

1. Visit the hospital's official website (we link to each hospital's international department directly from our hospital pages)
2. Call or email the hospital's **International Patient Department / International Medical Center** directly
3. Ask them: "Do you work with ShanghaiMed / Shanghai Keling Information Technology Co., Ltd.? Can you confirm an appointment if I am referred through them?"

The international departments at our partner hospitals are set up to handle exactly these inquiries — they receive them from embassies, insurance companies, and patients every day. If a hospital has never heard of the agency you are considering, that is a major red flag.

### Verify the doctor

We do not publish a fixed roster of doctors on this website, because **the right specialist depends on your specific condition** — not every neurosurgeon is right for your specific neurosurgical case, and we match you individually after reviewing your records.

What we do commit to, in writing before you pay any deposit:
- You receive your treating doctor's **full name, professional title, hospital affiliation, and credentials**
- We only recommend specialists at the rank of **Associate Chief Physician (副主任医师)** or above at Grade 3A hospitals
- You can — and are encouraged to — look up the doctor on the hospital's official website, on research databases like [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov), or by calling the hospital's international department to confirm the doctor's affiliation and appointment availability

If an agency refuses to give you the treating doctor's name before you pay, that is a red flag. If they cannot tell you the doctor's professional rank or department, that is a red flag. If they pressure you to pay quickly "to secure the doctor's slot" without letting you verify first, walk away.

## Step 3: Get the full cost breakdown in writing

Medical travel costs fall into two categories: **clinic/hospital fees** and **coordination/concierge fees**. Legitimate agencies keep these clearly separate.

**How we handle it:**
- Hospital and clinical fees are always **paid directly to the treating hospital** at the hospital's own rates. We do not collect, hold, or mark up medical fees, and we earn no commission from hospitals.
- Our concierge fees are published on our [Pricing page](#pricing) — from free initial consultation, to $50/hour standalone hospital escort, to packaged concierge from $500–$1,650 depending on your needs. There are no hidden markups on hospital bills.
- Before you pay any deposit, you receive a **written, itemised treatment plan and cost estimate** covering: concierge fees, estimated hospital charges, interpretation costs, accommodation estimates, and any known additional costs (e.g., special transport, additional companion hours).

**Questions to ask any agency:**
- Do you publish your service fees online, or only reveal them after a sales call?
- Are medical fees paid directly to the hospital, or does the agency collect them?
- If the agency collects fees on behalf of the hospital, do they provide an official hospital receipt/fapiao (发票)?
- What is included in the quoted price, and what could trigger additional charges?
- Is there a written refund policy, and does it clearly cover scenarios like visa denial or hospital rejection?

Our refund policy is published in full at [shanghaimedhealth.com/refund-policy](/refund-policy), in seven languages.

## Step 4: Confirm a written plan for complications and follow-up

Things do not always go perfectly, even at excellent hospitals. Flights get delayed. Treatment plans change. Patients develop complications. A competent agency has a plan for these situations before they happen.

**What we provide:**
- **Before arrival:** A written treatment plan including appointment schedule, estimated length of stay, contingency contact numbers (ours and the hospital's)
- **During your stay:** 24/7 emergency on-site support is available for urgent medical situations. For concierge package patients, we coordinate admission, family communication, and schedule changes as they arise
- **After you return home:** 30–60 days of remote post-care follow-up (depending on your package), including translation of discharge summaries and help communicating with your home-country doctor
- **Insurance coordination:** For patients with international insurance (Cigna, Bupa, Allianz, MSH, AXA, etc.), we assist with pre-authorisation and direct billing arrangements with the hospital

**Questions to ask any agency:**
- What happens if I develop complications after returning home?
- Will you help me communicate with my local doctor?
- Is post-treatment follow-up included in your fee or billed separately?
- What if my treatment takes longer than the planned stay?
- Do you help with insurance claims and direct billing?
